Privacy Policy

Hotel WhatsApp Booking Assistant · Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the Hotel WhatsApp Booking Assistant ("the Service"), operated by Aadarsh Sharma, handles information when you chat with it on WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • The messages you send to the Service.
  • Booking details you provide: name for the booking, check-in and check-out dates, number of guests and room type.

How we use it

  • To check room availability, prepare quotes and create booking requests.
  • To share your booking request with the hotel reception for approval, and to send you the confirmation, rejection or status messages.
  • To answer your questions about the hotel.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the hotel reception staff handling your booking, and with the service providers needed to run the Service: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), Google (Google Sheets for booking records and the Gemini AI model to generate replies) and our workflow automation host.

Storage and retention

Booking records are stored in a private Google Sheet. We keep them only as long as needed to manage bookings and for the hotel's record keeping.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by messaging the Service on WhatsApp or by emailing the contact below. We will delete your booking records and conversation data on request.

Security

Access to booking data is restricted to authorised staff and systems. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
